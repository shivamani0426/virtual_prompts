GOOD TO KNOW

Better air starts
with better knowledge.

01

Exposure ≠ emissions

A mask or purifier may lower what you breathe. A shared ride can reduce emissions. Both matter, in different ways.

02

Model, not a sensor

Forecasts estimate broad-area conditions. Local streets, traffic and indoor air may differ significantly.

03

Stay prepared

Use official local air-quality alerts, avoid indoor smoke, and seek medical care for serious symptoms.
